Crazy Car: Autonomous AI/RL RC Car

Rebuilt a commercial RC car with a custom microcontroller and precision servo steering. Constructed a 3D track environment in Unity, trained a Reinforcement Learning (RL) AI agent using ML-Agents (PPO), and deployed the trained policy to drive autonomously through physical track obstacles.

1. RC Car Hardware Conversion

I replaced stock consumer electronics on the RC vehicle chassis with an embedded microcontroller board. Added custom PWM motor driver bridges, high-torque steering servos, and optical wheel encoders for exact telemetry feedback.

2. Unity ML-Agents Reinforcement Learning Training

Modeled the vehicle physics and track layout inside Unity 3D. Utilizing Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), raycast distance sensors were used as state inputs. The AI learned optimal racing lines, throttle control, and drift recovery over 500,000 training steps.
