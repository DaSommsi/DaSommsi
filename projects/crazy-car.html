Project Timeline & Milestones

Oct 2025: Project Kickoff

Bought a €30 Lamborghini RC car from Amazon. Overhauled stock receiver with custom microcontroller wiring & servo steering mounts.

Nov - Dec 2025: Unity ML-Agents Training

Coded progressive learning track environments in Unity. Trained PPO AI agent taking velocity, IMU, and 3 ToF distance inputs to directly drive motor % and servo %.

Jan 2026: ESP32 Quantized Inference

Exported ONNX neural model to Int8 weights and wrote a custom C++ matrix calculator engine running directly on the ESP32 chip.

Feb 2026: Graz Competition & Debugging

Competed in Graz. Encountered signal loss due to missing IMU PCB traces & motor EMI interference. Spent 2 days troubleshooting root cause.

1. End-to-End Neural Network Control Architecture

Unlike classical robotics pipelines that use separate state estimators, path planners, and PID loops, we designed a direct end-to-end neural network policy. The model takes a 7-element state vector (vehicle velocity, 3-axis IMU pitch/roll/yaw rates, and 3 Time-of-Flight distance sensor readings) and directly predicts continuous control values for motor PWM percentage and steering servo angle.

Engineering Challenges & Root Cause Failures

The Graz Competition Failure: During custom PCB routing in SolidEdge, trace connections to the IMU sensor were accidentally omitted. When driving the car under high load at the Graz competition in Feb 2026, high motor current spikes generated severe Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) and voltage sags, crashing the ESP32 I2C sensor bus.

Key Lessons & What We Learned

  • Actuator Buffering: Always insert a PID or low-pass filter between raw AI neural network outputs and physical motor/servo drivers.
  • Timeline Discipline: Never finish hardware assembly 1 week before a competition. Start at least 6-8 months ahead!
  • Successor Action: Led directly to starting our 2027 Ground Effect Vehicle project 8 months early in April 2026.
