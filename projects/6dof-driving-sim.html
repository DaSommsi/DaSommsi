Project Timeline & Milestones

May 2025: Inspection & Diagnosis

Inspected non-functional legacy 6DoF rig at HTL Salzburg (2nd grade). Identified unshielded, bundled power & signal cables causing massive electromagnetic interference (EMI).

Summer - Autumn 2025: Hardware Re-Wiring & Fuse Box

Teammate designed custom power distribution fuse box. Ripped out unnecessary wiring and physically isolated high-power motor lines from delicate telemetry data cables.

Nov - Dec 2025: C++ Software Engine & Sponsorship

David programmed modular C++ control software. Successfully pitched Department Head to secure €2,000 display monitors and a €3,500 gaming rig.

Jan 2026: HTL Open Door Day Launch

Launched system at Open Door Day 2026 as the department's flagship exhibit. Proved a huge public success and solidified David's career focus in avionics & embedded electronics.

1. Physical Cable Isolation & Distribution Fuse Box

The original motion rig suffered from severe motor jitter and random emergency cut-outs. Our root cause investigation revealed that high-frequency PWM motor power cables were bundled directly alongside low-voltage serial encoder signal wires. We solved this by physically routing power lines along structural aluminum channels away from data lines, installing shielded differential signaling, and designing a custom fuse box.

2. Modular C++ Control Software & Inverse Kinematics

David authored the modular C++ control codebase connecting simulator telemetry (telemetry reader software interface) to motor controllers over CAN bus. Minor mathematical edge-case anomalies in extreme pitch/roll angles were documented, providing invaluable practical experience in writing scalable, high-performance C++ code.

Key Lessons & What We Learned

  • EMC Best Practices: Physical separation of high-power cables and low-voltage signal wires is non-negotiable in aerospace and motion platforms.
  • Clean Modular Architecture: Writing clean, scalable code with clear interface contracts saves hundreds of debugging hours.
  • Career Turning Point: Completing this project solidified David's determination to work in embedded hardware, flight control, and avionics.
