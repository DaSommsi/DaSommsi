Project Timeline & Milestones

April 2026: Concept & Sponsorship

Started design phase 8 months ahead of competition (direct lesson from Crazy Car). Secured €400 worth of sponsored components.

May - June 2026: SolidEdge CAD & Aerodynamics

David engineered the CAD chassis & ground-effect skirt concepts in SolidEdge, achieving 13 kg downforce cushion on a 2 kg vehicle mass.

July 2026 - Present: Simulink MPC & STM32H7 Integration

Developing Model Predictive Controller (MPC) in MATLAB/Simulink deployed to STM32H7 dual-core MCU for track position estimation & racing line optimization.

1. Aerodynamic Ground Cushion & Acceleration Tuning

Because competition tracks feature tight, technical turns where acceleration is far more critical than top speed, we engineered the vehicle for explosive Bugatti-level launch acceleration (capped at 18 km/h top speed). The ground effect fan generates 13 kg of downward suction, enabling extreme cornering G-forces without tire slip.

2. Simulink MPC & ToF Opponent Avoidance

David programmed the Model Predictive Control algorithm in MATLAB/Simulink to calculate optimal racing lines along the fixed circuit track based on wheel velocity inputs. Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors detect opponent vehicles, triggering dynamic line adjustments to secure victory at the 2027 competition.
