1. System Division & Team Responsibilities

Our team split responsibilities cleanly: my teammate focuses on carbon composite chassis mechanics, propulsion sizing, power distribution, and the 90° tilt mechanism. I serve as team lead and engineer all avionics, electronic redundancy, SORA safety compliance, ROS2 companion software, PID mixers, GCS ground control station, and military partner contacts.

2. Thermal IR AI Vision & Jamming-Safe Navigation

Designed for harsh Alpine environments, the drone carries a thermal IR camera streaming to an onboard AI model on the Nvidia Jetson. When a missing person is recognized, real-time GPS coordinates are transmitted back to the operator. To counter GPS jamming, an optical flow camera algorithm maps terrain features, bounding drift to within $\pm 2\text{m}$.
